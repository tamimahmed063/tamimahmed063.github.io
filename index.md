        <p>I am a Ph.D student in the School of <a href="https://school.eecs.wsu.edu/" target="_blank">EECS</a> at <a href="https://wsu.edu/" target="_blank">Washington State University</a>. I am working as a Graduate Research Assistant in the <a href="https://cps2rl.github.io/" target="_blank">Cyber-Physical Systems Security Research Lab (CPS2RL)</a>, advised by <a href="https://monowarhasan.info/" target="_blank">Dr. Monowar Hasan</a>.</p>
        <p>My research interests include real-time networking, systems security, and quantum-enable cyber-physical systems. Currently, I am working in scheduling and resource management for real-time networks particularly in time-sensitive networks (TSN). I am also interested in exploring real-world applications of TSN across emerging cyber-physical systems.
        <p>Before joining WSU in the Fall of 2024, I served as a Radio Service Engineer at Banglalink. I completed my undergraduate studies at the Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET).</p>
